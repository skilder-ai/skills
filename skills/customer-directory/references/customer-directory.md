## Status legend

- **Active** — current paying customer
- **At-risk** — flagged by account manager; escalation in flight
- **Inactive** — no current contract; do not contact without approval
- **Prospect** — in sales pipeline, not yet a customer

## Maintenance notes

- Update **Status** and **Last Interaction** after each meaningful touchpoint.
- Mark Notes as **Confidential** if they reference negotiation or churn risk.
